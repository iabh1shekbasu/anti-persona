Overview

Show a personalized vision–language model such as Yo’LLaVA or MyVLM a handful of your photos and it learns who you are: a new concept <sks> that it can spot in any new photo, just by being asked. Great for a personal assistant. A privacy risk for everyone else: anyone holding a few of your photos can teach a model to recognize you.

Personalization learns what your photos have in common. Anti-Persona takes it away.

We build identity prototypes from your other photos, in the vision encoder and in face space, and push each photo away from them. Small local cues can survive that, so Calibrated Evidence Suppression switches off any patch that still matches you more strongly than an unrelated photo would. Optimizing through JPEG with gradient smoothing keeps the protection alive after re-encoding.

Method

A personalized model learns who someone is from what their photos have in common. Anti-Persona adds a perturbation that removes exactly that shared identity evidence, in the vision encoder the model reads and in face space. The photo being protected is never its own reference: everything about the person is built from their other photos.

Components of Anti-Persona

Self repulsion

Away from the clean (own) photo.

Prototype repulsion

Away from the person’s other photos, in the vision encoder and in face space.

Calibrated evidence suppression

Switches off local identity matches.

Inside the optimization

The perturbation finds the face within ten steps. Scrub through the optimization: the protected image, the accumulated perturbation, and this step’s update without and with gradient smoothing (the one used is highlighted; smoothing, every third step, turns pixel speckle into blobs that survive JPEG).
